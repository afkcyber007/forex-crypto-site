Candlestick charts show price action in a clear, visual way and are used by almost every forex and crypto trader. Once you can read a candle, you can understand what buyers and sellers did in any market and on any timeframe.

## Anatomy of a candlestick

Each candle shows four prices for its period:

- **Open** – where price started
- **Close** – where price finished
- **High** – the highest price (top of the upper wick)
- **Low** – the lowest price (bottom of the lower wick)

A **green (bullish)** candle closed higher than it opened; a **red (bearish)** candle closed lower. The thick part is the **body**; the thin lines are the **wicks** (or shadows).

## Timeframes

The same market looks different on each timeframe:

| Timeframe | Each candle shows | Often used for |
|---|---|---|
| M5 / M15 | 5 / 15 minutes | Scalping, precise entries |
| H1 / H4 | 1 / 4 hours | Day and swing trading |
| D1 | 1 day | Trend direction, swing trades |

A common approach is to find the **trend on a higher timeframe** (H4 or D1) and the **entry on a lower one** (M15 or H1).

## 6 candlestick patterns worth knowing

1. **Bullish engulfing** – a green candle completely covers the previous red body. Buyers took control.
2. **Bearish engulfing** – a red candle covers the previous green body. Sellers took control.
3. **Pin bar (hammer / shooting star)** – a small body with a long wick, showing a strong rejection of a price level.
4. **Doji** – open and close are almost equal, showing indecision.
5. **Inside bar** – the whole candle sits inside the previous one, often before a breakout.
6. **Morning / evening star** – a three-candle reversal pattern at the end of a move.

## How to use patterns properly

Patterns work best with **context**:

- At **support or resistance** levels
- **With the trend** on the higher timeframe
- After the candle has **closed** — never trade a pattern that is still forming
- With a planned [stop loss and take profit](/guides/how-to-set-stop-loss-and-take-profit/)

## Frequently asked questions

### Are candlestick patterns reliable?

No pattern works every time. They are most useful combined with trend, key levels and good risk management.

### Which timeframe is best for beginners?

H1 and H4 are popular because they filter out a lot of noise while still offering several setups a week.

### Do candlestick charts work for crypto?

Yes — candlesticks work the same way for forex, gold, indices and crypto.
