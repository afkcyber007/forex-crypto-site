Nigeria is one of the most active crypto markets in the world, and USDT is the coin most traders use to fund exchanges and forex brokers. The usual way to buy it with naira is a **P2P bank transfer**, protected by the exchange's escrow.

Naira P2P is not currently offered on Binance or KuCoin, so Nigerian traders usually buy USDT on Bybit P2P and then trade or transfer it wherever they like. Local SEC-registered platforms such as Quidax and Busha also sell USDT for naira directly.

## Step-by-step: buy USDT with naira on P2P

1. **Sign up and verify.** Complete KYC with your NIN, international passport or driver's licence.
2. **Open P2P trading.** Select **Buy → USDT → NGN**.
3. **Set the payment method** to *Bank Transfer* and enter your amount in naira.
4. **Choose a reliable merchant** — high completion rate, many completed trades and a verified badge.
5. **Create the order.** The merchant's USDT is now held in escrow.
6. **Transfer the naira** from a bank account in your own name to the account shown in the order. NIP transfers usually arrive in seconds.
7. **Mark the order as paid.** When the merchant confirms, USDT is released to your Funding account.

## Stay safe

- Never trade outside the exchange's order page, even if a merchant asks.
- Make sure the name on the receiving bank account matches the merchant's verified name.
- Don't add words like "USDT" or "crypto" to your transfer narration.
- For large amounts, split your purchase across several smaller orders.

## Moving USDT to your broker or exchange

Copy the USDT deposit address and network (TRC20, BEP20 or another listed network) from your broker — for example an [Exness](/brokers/exness/) or [HFM](/brokers/hfm/) account — and withdraw USDT to it on the **same network**. Network mistakes are the most common reason for lost deposits, so always check twice.
