Moving crypto between exchanges, wallets and brokers is something every trader does — and the most common place beginners lose money is choosing the wrong network. Follow these steps and your transfers will arrive every time.

## The three things that must match

1. **The coin** – USDT to USDT, BTC to BTC.
2. **The network** – for example TRC20 (TRON), BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain), ERC20 (Ethereum), Solana or Arbitrum.
3. **The memo / tag** – required by some coins (such as XRP, XLM and TON deposits on many exchanges).

If any of these is wrong, the funds can be lost or need a long recovery process.

## Step-by-step: sending crypto

1. **On the receiving platform**, open **Deposit**, choose the coin and pick a network. Copy the **address** (and the **memo/tag** if shown).
2. **On the sending platform**, open **Withdraw**, choose the same coin and **exactly the same network**.
3. **Paste the address** — never type it by hand. Check the first and last few characters.
4. **Enter the memo/tag** if the receiving platform showed one.
5. **Send a small test amount first** when using a new address.
6. **Confirm** with your 2FA code and email confirmation.
7. **Track the transfer** with the transaction ID (TxID) on a blockchain explorer.

## Which network should I use?

| Network | Typical fee | Speed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TRC20 (TRON) | Low | Minutes | Very popular for USDT |
| BEP20 (BSC) | Very low | Minutes | Widely supported |
| Solana / Arbitrum / other L2s | Very low | Seconds–minutes | Check both sides support it |
| ERC20 (Ethereum) | Higher | Minutes | Use for larger amounts when required |

Fees change over time; the withdrawal page always shows the current fee and minimum withdrawal amount.

## Security tips

- Use an **address whitelist** so withdrawals can only go to saved addresses.
- Beware of **clipboard malware** — always re-check the pasted address.
- Never send crypto to someone who contacts you first offering "investment help".

## What if my transfer is stuck or missing?

1. Check the **TxID** on the blockchain explorer — if it shows "success", the funds reached the address.
2. Wait for the required **confirmations** on the receiving platform.
3. If the network or memo was wrong, contact the receiving platform's **support** with the TxID; some can recover funds.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a crypto transfer take?

Usually from seconds to about 30 minutes, depending on the network and the platforms' confirmation rules.

### Why is the withdrawal fee so high?

Fees depend on the network. Choosing TRC20, BEP20 or a layer-2 network is usually much cheaper than ERC20.

### Can I cancel a crypto transfer?

Once it is sent on the blockchain, no — which is why the test transaction matters.

*Always double-check addresses and networks. Crypto transactions cannot be reversed.*
