News releases cause many of the biggest moves in forex, gold, indices and even crypto. An economic calendar tells you exactly **when** these events happen, so you are never surprised by a sudden spike. Here is how to read and use it.

## What is an economic calendar?

It is a schedule of upcoming economic data releases and central bank events, with:

- **Date and time** (set it to your own time zone)
- **Currency or country** affected (USD, EUR, GBP…)
- **Impact level** – low, medium or high (often shown as colours or bulls/icons)
- **Previous**, **Forecast** and **Actual** values

Free calendars are available on TradingView, in many broker platforms and on financial news websites.

## The events that move markets most

| Event | What it measures | Usually released |
|---|---|---|
| **NFP** (Non-Farm Payrolls) | US jobs created | First Friday of the month, 8:30 a.m. New York time |
| **CPI** | Inflation | Monthly |
| **FOMC rate decision** | US interest rates | Eight times a year |
| **ECB / BoE / BoJ decisions** | Euro, pound, yen rates | Several times a year |
| **GDP** | Economic growth | Quarterly |
| **PMI** | Business activity | Monthly |

US data matters for almost everything, including gold, NAS100, US30 and Bitcoin.

## Forecast vs actual

Markets move on **surprises**. If actual inflation comes in **higher than forecast**, traders may expect higher interest rates, which often supports the currency. A number that matches the forecast usually causes a smaller reaction.

## How to trade around news safely

1. **Check the calendar every morning** and every Sunday for the week ahead.
2. **Avoid opening new trades** a few minutes before high-impact news.
3. **Expect wider spreads and slippage** around releases.
4. **Reduce your size or tighten risk** on open trades if needed.
5. **Wait for the first spike to settle** — the clearer move often comes 15–30 minutes later.

Pair this with solid [stop losses](/guides/how-to-set-stop-loss-and-take-profit/) and correct [lot sizes](/guides/how-to-calculate-lot-size-in-forex/).

## Does news affect crypto?

Yes. Major US releases such as CPI and FOMC decisions often move Bitcoin and the wider crypto market, because they change expectations for interest rates and risk appetite.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which time zone does the calendar use?

Most calendars let you choose your own time zone — set it once so every event shows your local time.

### Is news trading good for beginners?

News moves are fast and unpredictable. Beginners usually do better by staying out during high-impact releases and trading the calmer moves afterwards.

### What does "high impact" mean?

It marks events that historically cause the largest price moves, such as NFP, CPI and central bank decisions.

*Trading around news is high risk. Prices can gap and stop losses may be filled at worse prices.*
