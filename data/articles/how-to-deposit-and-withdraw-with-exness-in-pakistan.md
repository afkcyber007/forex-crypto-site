## Payment methods Exness offers in Pakistan

When you open the **Deposit** page in your Exness Personal Area with a Pakistani profile, you will usually see these options:

| Method | Currency | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| JazzCash | PKR | Small and medium deposits from your phone |
| Easypaisa | PKR | Small and medium deposits from your phone |

| Cards, Skrill, Neteller | USD / PKR | Traders who already use them |

The exact list, limits and processing times are always shown on the deposit page itself, because they depend on your country and verification level. Exness does not charge its own fee on most deposit and withdrawal methods.

## Before your first deposit

1. **Open your account** — follow our step-by-step [Exness sign-up guide](/brokers/exness/sign-up/).
2. **Verify your profile** — upload your CNIC (or passport) and a proof of address. Verification unlocks all payment methods and higher limits.
3. **Use your own wallet** — the JazzCash or Easypaisa account must be registered in the same name as your Exness profile. Payments from someone else's wallet can be returned.

## How to deposit with JazzCash or Easypaisa

1. Log in to the Exness Personal Area (website or Exness Trade app).
2. Tap **Deposit** and choose **JazzCash** or **Easypaisa**.
3. Select the trading account you want to fund and enter the amount in PKR.
4. Confirm. You will be sent to the wallet's payment page or receive a prompt in your JazzCash / Easypaisa app.
5. Approve the payment with your MPIN. The balance usually appears in your trading account within minutes.

The standard account minimum deposit at Exness is $10, but the minimum for each payment method is shown on the method's page.

## How to deposit with USDT (TRC20)

USDT is the most flexible option — it works at any hour and for any amount.

1. In **Deposit**, choose **Tether (USDT TRC20)**.
2. Copy the wallet address shown (or scan the QR code).
3. From your exchange, send USDT on the **TRON (TRC20)** network to that address. Always double-check that the network matches.
4. The deposit is credited after the network confirmations, usually within minutes.

Don't have USDT yet? See [how to buy USDT in Pakistan](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-pakistan/) with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a bank transfer.

## How to withdraw from Exness in Pakistan

1. Close any open trades you want to withdraw from, or make sure you keep enough free margin.
2. Go to **Withdrawal** in the Personal Area.
3. Choose the method. Exness generally asks you to withdraw back to the same method you deposited with first — this is a standard anti-money-laundering rule, not a restriction on your profits.
4. Enter the amount, confirm with the code sent to your phone or email, and submit.

Many Exness withdrawals are processed automatically, so wallet and crypto payouts often arrive the same day.

## Tips for smooth payments

- **Verify early.** Complete verification before you deposit, so withdrawals are never delayed.
- **Keep wallet limits in mind.** JazzCash and Easypaisa have daily and monthly limits set by the wallet provider. For larger amounts, use a bank transfer or USDT.
- **Use one method.** Depositing and withdrawing through the same channel keeps things simple.
- **Save your transaction IDs** until the money arrives.
