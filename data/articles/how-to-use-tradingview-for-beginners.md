TradingView is one of the most popular charting platforms in the world, used for forex, gold, indices and crypto. It runs in your browser and on your phone, and the free plan is enough to get started. Here is how to use it step by step.

## Step 1 – Create a free account

Sign up on TradingView with your email or Google account. The free **Basic** plan gives you full access to charts, drawing tools and a number of indicators and alerts. Paid plans add more indicators per chart, more alerts and multi-chart layouts.

## Step 2 – Open a chart

1. Click **Products → Supercharts** (or open the app).
2. Type a symbol in the search box, for example **EURUSD**, **XAUUSD**, **NAS100** or **BTCUSDT**.
3. Choose a **timeframe** at the top (15m, 1h, 4h, 1D).
4. Choose **Candles** as the chart type — learn [how to read candlestick charts](/guides/how-to-read-candlestick-charts/).

## Step 3 – Draw support and resistance

Use the **horizontal line** tool on the left toolbar to mark levels where price has turned several times. Use the **trend line** tool to connect higher lows in an uptrend or lower highs in a downtrend. These levels help you plan entries, [stop losses and take profits](/guides/how-to-set-stop-loss-and-take-profit/).

## Step 4 – Add indicators

Click **Indicators** and search for:

- **Moving Average (EMA 50 / 200)** – trend direction
- **RSI** – momentum and overbought/oversold conditions
- **ATR** – volatility, useful for stop-loss distance
- **Volume** – especially useful for crypto

Keep it simple: two or three indicators are usually enough.

## Step 5 – Set price alerts

Right-click on a price level and choose **Add alert**. TradingView can notify you by app, email or pop-up when price reaches your level, so you don't have to watch the screen all day.

## Step 6 – Trade from TradingView

Many brokers and exchanges connect directly to TradingView, so you can place orders from the chart. Among our partners, [FP Markets](/brokers/fp-markets/), [Eightcap](/brokers/eightcap/), [Vantage](/brokers/vantage/) and [PU Prime](/brokers/pu-prime/) offer TradingView integration for forex and CFDs, and crypto exchanges such as [Bitunix](/exchanges/bitunix/) use TradingView-powered charts in their own platform.

## Useful TradingView tips

- **Save a layout** so your indicators and drawings load every time.
- **Use the watchlist** for your favourite pairs.
- **Use the replay mode** to practise on past price action.
- **Check the economic calendar** tab before news — see [how to read the economic calendar](/guides/how-to-read-the-economic-calendar/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is TradingView free?

Yes, the Basic plan is free. Paid plans add more indicators, alerts and layouts.

### Can I trade directly on TradingView?

Yes, if your broker or exchange is integrated. Otherwise, use TradingView for analysis and place trades in your broker's platform.

### Is TradingView better than MT5?

TradingView has stronger charting and social features; MT5 is the standard trading platform at most forex brokers. Many traders use both.
