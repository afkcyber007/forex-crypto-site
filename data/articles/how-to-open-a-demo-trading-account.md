A demo account lets you trade real market prices with **virtual money**. It is the safest way to learn a platform, test a strategy and build confidence before you risk real funds. Here is how to open one for forex and for crypto.

## Why every trader should start with a demo

- **No risk:** mistakes cost nothing.
- **Real prices:** demo accounts use live market quotes.
- **Learn the platform:** placing orders, setting stops and reading charts become second nature.
- **Test strategies:** see whether an idea works before you fund it.

## How to open a forex demo account (MT4 / MT5)

1. **Pick a broker.** Choose one you could later trade with for real — see the [best forex brokers](/best-forex-broker/).
2. **Register.** Click Visit Site on the broker's review, for example [Exness](/brokers/exness/) or [Vantage](/brokers/vantage/), and create your profile with your email.
3. **Choose "Demo account"** in the client area. Select **MT5** (recommended for new traders) or MT4 — see [MT4 vs MT5](/guides/mt4-vs-mt5/).
4. **Set your virtual balance and leverage.** Use a balance similar to what you plan to deposit later, not an unrealistic $100,000.
5. **Download MetaTrader** on your phone or computer and log in with the demo account number, password and server shown in your client area.
6. **Start practising.** Open small trades on EUR/USD or gold and always set a stop loss.

## How to use demo trading on a crypto exchange

Many exchanges have a built-in demo or "paper trading" mode for futures:

1. Create an account on an exchange such as [Bitunix](/exchanges/bitunix/), [WEEX](/exchanges/weex/) or [Bybit](/exchanges/bybit/).
2. Open the futures section and switch to **Demo trading** (usually a toggle in the menu or the account settings).
3. You receive virtual USDT to practise long and short positions, leverage and stop losses.

## Make your demo practice count

- **Trade the same size** you will use live.
- **Keep a journal** of every trade: entry, stop, target and result.
- **Set a goal**, for example 30–50 trades following your rules.
- **Practise risk control** — read our [risk management guide](/guides/risk-management-for-traders/).

## When should you switch to a live account?

Move to a small live account when you can follow your plan consistently for several weeks and you understand [how to calculate lot size](/guides/how-to-calculate-lot-size-in-forex/). Start with the smallest sizes — trading real money feels different, and that experience is part of the learning.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a demo account free?

Yes. Demo accounts are free at every broker and exchange we list.

### Does a demo account expire?

Some brokers close inactive demo accounts after a period of time, but you can always open a new one.

### Is demo trading the same as live trading?

Prices are the same, but live execution can include slippage, and emotions are stronger with real money.

*Trading with leverage is high risk. Practise on demo before trading real funds.*
