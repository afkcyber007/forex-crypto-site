MetaTrader 4 (MT4) and MetaTrader 5 (MT5) are the two most popular retail trading platforms in the world. Both are free and offered by every broker we review. Here is how they differ.

## The short answer

- Choose **MT5** if you are starting today. It is faster, has more timeframes and tools, and is where development is focused.
- Choose **MT4** only if you rely on a specific indicator or Expert Advisor that exists only for MT4.

## Key differences

- **Timeframes** — MT4 has 9; MT5 has 21, including 2-hour, 3-hour and 8-hour charts.
- **Programming language** — MT4 uses MQL4 and MT5 uses MQL5. Code is not directly compatible.
- **Backtesting** — MT5's strategy tester is multi-threaded and supports real-tick testing, so it is much faster and more accurate for EAs.
- **Order types** — MT5 adds Buy Stop Limit and Sell Stop Limit orders.
- **Economic calendar** — built into MT5.
- **Markets** — MT5 was designed for multiple asset classes, and brokers often offer more stocks and indices on MT5.

## Expert Advisors (trading robots)

Both platforms run EAs. If you buy or download an EA, check which platform it was written for. When testing a robot, use MT5's real-tick backtesting and then forward-test on a demo account for several weeks before going live.

## Alternatives

cTrader (offered by IC Markets) is popular with scalpers for its depth-of-market view, and TradingView (offered by IC Markets and Vantage) is ideal if you do your analysis there.
