NAS100 (the Nasdaq 100) and US30 (the Dow Jones Industrial Average) are two of the most traded indices in the world. They move fast, trade almost 24 hours on weekdays and are available at every forex broker we list. Here is how to trade them.

## What are NAS100 and US30?

- **NAS100** tracks the 100 largest non-financial companies on the Nasdaq exchange — heavy in technology names such as Apple, Microsoft and Nvidia.
- **US30** tracks 30 large US blue-chip companies across different industries.

With a forex broker you trade them as **CFDs**, which lets you go long or short with leverage and small position sizes.

## What moves the indices?

- **US interest rates and Federal Reserve decisions**
- **Inflation data (CPI)** and **jobs reports (NFP)**
- **Earnings** of the largest companies (especially for NAS100)
- **Overall risk sentiment** in global markets

Follow these events on the [economic calendar](/guides/how-to-read-the-economic-calendar/).

## Best times to trade

The busiest period is the **US cash session**, which opens at **9:30 a.m. New York time**. The first one to two hours after the open usually bring the biggest moves and the tightest spreads. Index CFDs also trade outside these hours, but with less volume.

## Contract size and lot size

Contract sizes differ between brokers — for example, 1 lot might be worth $1 per index point at one broker and a different amount at another. Always check the **contract specification** in your platform, then use the lot size formula:

> Lot size = Risk amount ÷ (Stop distance in points × Value per point per lot)

See [how to calculate lot size](/guides/how-to-calculate-lot-size-in-forex/) for worked examples.

## A simple approach for beginners

1. Mark the **previous day's high and low** and key levels on the H1 chart.
2. Wait for the **US open** and let the first 15–30 minutes settle.
3. Trade **in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend**, using a clear candlestick signal.
4. Always place a [stop loss](/guides/how-to-set-stop-loss-and-take-profit/) and risk no more than 1% per trade.
5. Avoid opening new trades just before major news.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is NAS100 more volatile than US30?

NAS100 often moves more in percentage terms because of its technology focus, but both can move quickly around news.

### What is the minimum amount to trade indices?

With 0.01 lots at a low-deposit broker, you can start with a small account — just keep the risk per trade small.

### Can I hold index trades overnight?

Yes, but you will pay or receive an overnight swap, and prices can gap at the open.

*CFDs are complex instruments with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage.*
