## The rules in India

- **Exchanges must register with FIU-IND.** Crypto platforms serving Indian users have to register with the Financial Intelligence Unit and follow anti-money-laundering rules. Binance completed FIU-IND registration in 2024.
- **30% tax on gains.** Profits from selling or swapping crypto (Virtual Digital Assets) are taxed at a flat 30% plus 4% cess. Only the purchase cost can be deducted, and losses cannot be set off against other income.
- **1% TDS.** A 1% Tax Deducted at Source applies when crypto is transferred above the yearly threshold. Indian-registered platforms deduct it automatically.
- Budget 2026 kept these rates unchanged and added penalties for missed reporting, so keep good records.

*This is general information, not tax advice — speak to a tax professional for your situation.*

## Step-by-step: buying USDT via P2P

1. **Verify your account** with PAN and Aadhaar or passport.
2. Open **P2P → Buy → USDT → INR** and filter by **UPI** or **IMPS**.
3. Choose a merchant with a high completion rate and many trades.
4. Place the order — the USDT is locked in escrow.
5. Pay from your **own** bank or UPI ID to the details in the order.
6. Mark as paid. The merchant releases USDT to your wallet, usually within minutes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I pay tax when I buy USDT?

Buying is not a taxable gain, but the 1% TDS rules apply to transfers above the threshold. Tax is due on profit when you sell or swap.

### Can I use UPI to buy crypto?

Yes — UPI is the most common payment method on Indian P2P markets and Indian exchanges.
