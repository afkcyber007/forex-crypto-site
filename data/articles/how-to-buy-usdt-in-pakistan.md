USDT (Tether) is the easiest way for traders in Pakistan to fund a crypto exchange or a forex broker. Because exchanges do not yet take rupee bank deposits directly, almost everyone buys USDT through **P2P (peer-to-peer)** markets, paying the seller with JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or a bank transfer.

## Is buying crypto legal in Pakistan?

Pakistan's Virtual Assets Act 2026 set up the **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA)** as the crypto regulator. Licensing is still being rolled out: Binance received a PVARA No-Objection Certificate in December 2025, the first step towards a local licence. Until full licences are issued, rupee buyers use the P2P markets of international exchanges.

## Step-by-step: buy USDT with P2P

1. **Create and verify your account.** Sign up on the exchange and complete identity verification (KYC) with your CNIC. P2P requires a verified account.
2. **Open P2P.** Go to *Buy crypto → P2P*, select **Buy**, **USDT** and currency **PKR**.
3. **Filter by payment method.** Choose JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or Bank Transfer and enter the amount in rupees.
4. **Pick a trusted seller.** Look for a high completion rate (95%+), hundreds of completed orders and a verified-merchant badge.
5. **Place the order.** The seller's USDT is locked in the exchange's **escrow**, so it cannot disappear while you pay.
6. **Pay from your own account** to the details shown in the order, within the time limit. Do not write "crypto" or "USDT" in the payment note.
7. **Tap "Transferred, notify seller".** The seller confirms the money arrived and releases the USDT to your Funding wallet — usually within a few minutes.

## Safety rules for P2P in Pakistan

- Only pay **inside an open order** — never send money to someone who contacts you on WhatsApp or Telegram.
- Pay only from an account **in your own name**, and only to the name shown in the order.
- Never release or confirm anything before the money is actually in your account (when selling).
- Keep screenshots of every payment until the order is completed. If anything goes wrong, use the **Appeal** button — support can see the escrowed funds.

## Send USDT to your broker or exchange

1. On the destination (for example your [Exness](/brokers/exness/) deposit page or your Bitunix account), copy the **USDT deposit address** and note the network — usually **TRC20** or **BEP20**.
2. On the exchange where you bought, choose *Withdraw → USDT*, paste the address and select the **same network**.
3. Confirm. TRC20 and BEP20 transfers normally arrive within minutes for a small network fee.
