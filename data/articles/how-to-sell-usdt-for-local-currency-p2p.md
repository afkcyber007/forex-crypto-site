After a profitable trade you will want your money in your bank account or mobile wallet. In most countries the simplest way is to **sell USDT on a P2P market**, where a buyer pays you in local currency and the exchange holds your USDT in escrow until you confirm the payment.

## Before you sell

- Move your USDT to the exchange's **Funding / P2P wallet**.
- Add a payment method **in your own name** — bank account, JazzCash, Easypaisa, UPI, M-Pesa, GCash or whatever your country uses.
- Complete identity verification; it is required for P2P.

## Step-by-step: selling USDT

1. Open **P2P → Sell → USDT** and choose your local currency.
2. Filter by your payment method and enter the amount.
3. Choose a buyer with a high completion rate and many completed orders.
4. Create the order — your USDT is locked in escrow.
5. **Wait until the money is really in your account.** Open your banking or wallet app and check the balance yourself.
6. Only then tap **Release**. The buyer receives the USDT and the order is complete.

## The four golden rules for sellers

1. **Never release on a screenshot.** Fake payment screenshots and fake SMS alerts are the most common scam. Check your own app.
2. **The payer's name must match** the buyer's verified name on the exchange. If a third party pays, cancel and appeal.
3. **Stay inside the platform.** Never move the conversation to WhatsApp or Telegram.
4. **Use Appeal** if anything looks wrong — support can see and hold the escrowed funds.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a P2P sale take?

Usually 5–20 minutes with an active buyer and an instant payment method.

### Are there fees for P2P?

Many big exchanges charge no P2P fee for regular users. Your real price is the rate the buyer offers, so compare a few offers.

### Do I need to pay tax?

Tax rules depend on your country. Keep a record of every sale.
