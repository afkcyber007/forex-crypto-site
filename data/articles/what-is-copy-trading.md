Copy trading lets you automatically copy the trades of an experienced trader in your own account. When they open a trade, your account opens the same trade in proportion to the money you allocate. When they close it, yours closes too. It is one of the most popular ways for beginners to take part in forex and crypto markets while they learn.

## How copy trading works

1. **Choose a trader (the "lead" or "master").** Platforms show each trader's history: return, maximum drawdown, number of followers, how long they have traded and which markets they trade.
2. **Allocate an amount.** For example, you put $200 into copying one trader.
3. **Trades are copied automatically.** If the trader risks 2% of their account on a trade, your copy risks about 2% of your $200.
4. **Stop at any time.** You can stop copying and close the copied positions whenever you want.

## What does copy trading cost?

Most platforms do not charge a separate fee to copy. Instead, the trader you copy usually receives a **share of the profits** you make from copying them — commonly somewhere around 10–20%, depending on the platform and the trader. You still pay normal trading costs (spreads or trading fees).

## How to choose a trader to copy

- **Look at at least 3–6 months of history.** One great week means very little.
- **Check the maximum drawdown.** This is the biggest fall from a peak. Lower is safer.
- **Prefer steady returns over huge ones.** A trader showing +500% in a month is usually taking extreme risks.
- **Check the leverage they use.** High leverage can wipe out an account in one bad move.
- **Spread your money.** Copying 2–3 different traders reduces the impact of one bad trader.

## Copy trading tips for beginners

- Start with a small amount you can afford to lose.
- Set a **copy stop-loss** (most platforms let you stop copying automatically if losses reach a set amount).
- Review the traders you copy every week.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is copy trading good for beginners?
Yes, it is one of the easiest ways to start, as long as you choose traders carefully, start small and use a copy stop-loss.

### How much money do I need to start copy trading?
Many crypto exchanges let you start copying with around $10–$100. Forex copy-trading services usually start from about $50–$200.

### Can I lose money copy trading?
Yes. If the trader you copy loses, you lose too. That is why diversification and stop-losses matter.
