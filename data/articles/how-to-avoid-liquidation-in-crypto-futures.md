Liquidation happens when a leveraged position loses so much that the exchange closes it automatically. It is the most common way futures traders lose their money — and it is almost always avoidable.

## What is liquidation?

When you open a futures position, you put up **margin**. If the price moves against you and your margin falls below the **maintenance margin**, the exchange closes the position at the **liquidation price**, and the margin is lost.

## How leverage moves your liquidation price

With **isolated margin**, the liquidation price is roughly this far from your entry (a little closer once fees and maintenance margin are included):

| Leverage | Price move to liquidation (approx.) |
|---|---|
| 2x | ~50% |
| 5x | ~20% |
| 10x | ~10% |
| 25x | ~4% |
| 50x | ~2% |
| 100x | ~1% |

Bitcoin can move 1–2% in minutes — which is why very high leverage is so risky. Your exchange shows the exact liquidation price before you open a trade.

## 8 rules to avoid liquidation

1. **Use low leverage.** Most experienced traders stay between 2x and 10x.
2. **Always set a stop loss** well before the liquidation price — see [how to set stop loss and take profit](/guides/how-to-set-stop-loss-and-take-profit/).
3. **Use isolated margin** so one bad trade cannot drain your whole account.
4. **Risk only 1–2% per trade.** Calculate size from your stop distance, not from the leverage slider.
5. **Avoid trading right before major news** such as CPI or FOMC — see the [economic calendar guide](/guides/how-to-read-the-economic-calendar/).
6. **Don't add margin to a losing trade** just to avoid liquidation — accept the planned loss.
7. **Watch funding rates** on positions you hold for days.
8. **Keep a journal** and review every liquidation or big loss.

## Isolated vs cross margin

- **Isolated:** each position has its own margin. Maximum loss = that margin.
- **Cross:** all positions share your full futures balance. Positions survive longer, but a big move can liquidate the whole balance.

Beginners are usually safer with isolated margin.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I lose more than my margin?

On major exchanges, retail losses are generally limited to the margin through liquidation and the exchange's insurance fund.

### What leverage is safest?

Lower is safer. Many traders use 2x–5x on Bitcoin and even less on volatile altcoins.

### Does a stop loss prevent liquidation?

A stop loss placed before the liquidation price closes the trade at your chosen loss instead — it is your most important protection.

*Crypto futures are high risk. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.*
