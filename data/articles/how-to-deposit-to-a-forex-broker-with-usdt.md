Paying with USDT is the fastest way to fund a forex account from almost any country: it works 24/7, costs only a small network fee and usually arrives within minutes. Here is exactly how to do it.

## Why use USDT?

- **Works worldwide**, even where local bank transfers to brokers are slow.
- **Fast:** TRC20 and BEP20 transfers normally confirm in a few minutes.
- **Cheap:** you pay a small blockchain fee instead of bank or card charges.
- **Easy withdrawals:** most brokers pay profits back to the same USDT address.

## Step 1 — Get USDT

Buy USDT on an exchange such as [Bitunix](/exchanges/bitunix/), [Bybit](/exchanges/bybit/) or [Binance](/exchanges/binance/). Country guides: [Pakistan](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-pakistan/), [Nigeria](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-nigeria/), [India](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-india/).

## Step 2 — Copy your broker's deposit address

1. Log in to your broker's client area and open **Deposit**.
2. Choose **USDT** and the network, for example **TRC20 (TRON)**.
3. Select the trading account and copy the wallet address.

## Step 3 — Send from the exchange

1. On the exchange, go to **Withdraw → USDT**.
2. Paste the broker's address and choose **exactly the same network**.
3. Enter the amount and confirm with your 2FA code.

> **Golden rule:** the network on both sides must match. Sending TRC20 USDT to a BEP20 address (or the other way round) can lose the funds. If you are unsure, send a small test first.

## Step 4 — Check your trading account

The deposit appears after the blockchain confirmations — usually within minutes. If the broker credits your account in USD, 1 USDT is normally converted at close to 1 USD.

## Withdrawing profits back to USDT

Open **Withdrawal**, choose USDT on the same network, paste your exchange's USDT deposit address and confirm. You can then sell USDT for local currency through P2P — see [how to sell USDT for local currency](/guides/how-to-sell-usdt-for-local-currency-p2p/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which network is cheapest?

TRC20 and BEP20 usually have the lowest fees. Use whichever both your exchange and broker support.

### Is there a minimum USDT deposit?

Yes, each broker sets one; it is shown on the deposit page.
