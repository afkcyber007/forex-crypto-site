Buying Bitcoin for the first time is easier than most people think — it takes about 15 minutes on a good exchange. This guide walks you through every step and shows you how to keep your coins safe.

## Step 2 – Create and secure your account

1. Click **Visit Site** and register with your email or phone.
2. Use a **strong, unique password**.
3. Turn on **2FA** with an authenticator app.
4. Complete **identity verification** (a photo of your ID and a selfie) — required for card and P2P purchases.

## Step 3 – Choose how to pay

- **Debit or credit card** – the fastest option, usually with a slightly higher fee.
- **Bank transfer** – lower fees where local bank deposits are supported.
- **P2P (peer-to-peer)** – pay another user in your local currency with bank transfer or mobile wallet; the exchange holds the crypto in escrow. Ideal in countries where card or bank deposits are limited. See our guides for [Pakistan](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-pakistan/), [Nigeria](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-nigeria/) and [India](/guides/how-to-buy-usdt-in-india/).
- **Buy USDT first** – in many countries it is easiest to buy USDT via P2P and then swap it for BTC on the spot market.

## Step 4 – Buy your Bitcoin

1. Open **Buy Crypto** (card / P2P) or the **Spot** market (BTC/USDT).
2. Enter the amount in your currency or in USDT.
3. Check the price and fee, then confirm.

You don't need to buy a whole Bitcoin — you can buy a small fraction. One Bitcoin divides into 100 million units called **satoshis**.

## Step 5 – Store it safely

- For small amounts and regular trading, keeping BTC on a reputable exchange with 2FA is convenient.
- For long-term savings, many people move coins to a **personal wallet** — always copy the address carefully and send a small test first. See [how to transfer crypto between exchanges and wallets](/guides/how-to-transfer-crypto-between-exchanges/).

Read our [crypto exchange safety guide](/guides/crypto-exchange-safety/) for more security tips.

## Tips for first-time buyers

- **Start small** and learn how buying, sending and selling work.
- **Consider buying regularly** (for example weekly) instead of all at once.
- **Ignore "guaranteed profit" offers** on social media — they are scams.
- **Keep records** of your purchases for tax purposes in your country.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum amount of Bitcoin I can buy?

Most exchanges let you start with just a few dollars' worth.

### Is it safe to buy Bitcoin online?

Yes, when you use a reputable exchange, enable 2FA and never share your passwords or codes.

### Can I buy Bitcoin with cash?

Through P2P markets you can pay sellers with local payment methods; some regions also have Bitcoin ATMs.

*Crypto prices are volatile and you can lose money. This guide is for education only.*
