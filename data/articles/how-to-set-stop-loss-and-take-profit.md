A stop loss protects your account; a take profit locks in gains. Traders who use both consistently survive long enough to improve. This guide shows where to place them and how to set them on your platform.

## What are stop loss and take profit?

- **Stop loss (SL):** an order that closes your trade automatically at a set loss.
- **Take profit (TP):** an order that closes your trade automatically at a set profit.

Both are placed **before** or right after you open the trade, so emotions don't make the decision for you.

## Where to place your stop loss

Place the stop where your trade idea is **proven wrong**, not at a random number of pips:

- **Below the last swing low** when buying, or **above the last swing high** when selling.
- **Beyond support or resistance**, with a small buffer for the spread.
- **Using volatility:** many traders use 1–1.5 × the ATR indicator so normal price noise doesn't stop them out.

Then adjust your **lot size** so the stop equals 1–2% of your account — see [how to calculate lot size](/guides/how-to-calculate-lot-size-in-forex/).

## Where to place your take profit

- At the **next support or resistance** level.
- Using a **risk-to-reward ratio** of at least **1:1.5 or 1:2** — if you risk 20 pips, aim for 30–40 pips.
- With **partial profits**: close half at TP1, move the stop to break-even and let the rest run to TP2.

| Risk : Reward | Win rate needed to break even |
|---|---|
| 1 : 1 | 50% |
| 1 : 2 | 33% |
| 1 : 3 | 25% |

## How to set SL and TP on MT4 / MT5

1. Open a **New Order** (or tap the + icon on mobile).
2. Choose the symbol and volume (lot size).
3. Enter the **Stop Loss** and **Take Profit** prices.
4. Click **Buy** or **Sell**.
5. To change them later, double-click the open trade (or long-press on mobile) and choose **Modify**.

## How to set SL and TP on a crypto exchange

On futures platforms such as [Bitunix](/exchanges/bitunix/), [WEEX](/exchanges/weex/) or [BloFin](/exchanges/blofin/):

1. Open the order panel and enter your size.
2. Tick **TP/SL** and enter the trigger prices (or a percentage).
3. Place the order — the TP/SL is attached to the position automatically.

A stop loss is your best protection against [liquidation](/guides/how-to-avoid-liquidation-in-crypto-futures/).

## Trailing stops

A **trailing stop** follows the price as it moves in your favour, locking in profit while giving the trade room to run. MT4/MT5 and most crypto exchanges support trailing stops.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I always use a stop loss?

Yes. A single trade without a stop can wipe out weeks of gains.

### Why was my stop loss hit before price reached it on the chart?

Charts usually show the bid price, while buy positions close on the bid and sell positions close on the ask — the spread can trigger the stop slightly earlier.

### Can I move my stop loss?

Moving it closer to lock in profit is fine. Moving it further away to avoid a loss is one of the most common beginner mistakes.

*Leveraged trading is high risk. Stop losses may be filled at a worse price during fast markets.*
