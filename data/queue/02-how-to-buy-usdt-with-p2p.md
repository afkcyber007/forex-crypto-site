USDT (Tether) is the most popular stablecoin in the world. It is designed to stay close to $1, which makes it the standard way to fund a crypto exchange or a forex broker that accepts crypto deposits. The easiest way to buy USDT in most countries is **P2P (peer-to-peer) trading**.

## What is P2P trading?

On a P2P marketplace you buy USDT directly from another user and pay them in your local currency — by bank transfer, mobile wallet or other local payment method. The exchange holds the seller's USDT in **escrow** until the seller confirms they have received your payment, and then releases it to you. This protects both sides.

P2P marketplaces are available on [Binance](/exchanges/binance/), [Bybit](/exchanges/bybit/), [BingX](/exchanges/bingx/), [MEXC](/exchanges/mexc/) and [KuCoin](/exchanges/kucoin/).

## How to buy USDT with P2P (step by step)

1. **Create and verify your exchange account.** P2P requires identity verification (KYC).
2. **Open the P2P section** and choose **Buy → USDT**.
3. **Select your currency and payment method** — for example your bank or a local mobile wallet.
4. **Pick a seller.** Choose one with a high completion rate (95%+), many completed orders and a good price.
5. **Enter the amount** and create the order. The seller's USDT is now locked in escrow.
6. **Pay the seller** using the exact details shown in the order, within the time limit.
7. **Click "Transferred, notify seller".** Only do this after you have actually paid.
8. **Receive your USDT.** The seller confirms payment and the USDT is released to your funding wallet, usually within minutes.

## P2P safety rules

- **Never pay outside the platform's order page** or chat with sellers on Telegram or WhatsApp.
- **Never click "paid" before paying.** This can get your account restricted.
- **Use your own bank account** in your own name.
- **Do not write "crypto" or "USDT" in the payment reference** unless the seller asks for a specific reference shown in the order.
- **If something goes wrong, open an appeal** inside the order. Exchange support will review the evidence.

## After you buy USDT

- **Trade crypto:** move USDT from your funding wallet to your spot or futures wallet.
- **Fund a forex broker:** many brokers accept USDT deposits. Always choose the **same network** (for example TRC20 or BEP20) on both sides, or your funds can be lost.
- **Withdraw to your own wallet** for long-term storage.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is P2P trading safe?
Yes, when you follow the rules above. The escrow system protects your money as long as you stay inside the official order page.

### What is the minimum amount of USDT I can buy?
It depends on the seller — many accept orders from the equivalent of $10–$20.

### Which network should I use to send USDT?
TRC20 (Tron) and BEP20 (BNB Chain) are cheap and fast. The important thing is to use the same network for sending and receiving.
