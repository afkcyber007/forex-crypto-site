Where you keep your crypto matters as much as what you buy. There are two main choices: leaving it in your **crypto exchange wallet** or moving it to a **hardware wallet**. Most experienced traders use both.

## What is a crypto exchange wallet?

When you deposit or buy crypto on an exchange such as [Binance](/exchanges/binance/), [Bybit](/exchanges/bybit/) or [Bitunix](/exchanges/bitunix/), it sits in your **exchange wallet** (often split into funding, spot and futures accounts). The exchange holds the keys for you.

**Advantages**
- Instant trading — no transfers needed.
- Easy recovery if you forget your password.
- Built-in features like Earn, P2P and copy trading.

## What is a hardware wallet?

A hardware wallet (also called a **cold wallet**) is a small physical device that keeps your private keys offline. Only you control the funds, protected by a PIN and a **recovery phrase** (seed phrase).

**Advantages**
- Your keys never touch the internet.
- You are fully in control of your coins.
- Ideal for long-term savings.

## Exchange wallet vs hardware wallet

| | Exchange wallet | Hardware wallet |
|---|---|---|
| Who holds the keys | The exchange | You |
| Best for | Active trading | Long-term holding |
| Speed | Instant | Needs a transfer to trade |
| Cost | Free | Device purchase |
| Recovery | Account recovery via support | Only with your seed phrase |

## What about Web3 wallets?

Some exchanges, including Binance and Bybit, also offer a **Web3 wallet** inside their app. It is a self-custody wallet — you hold the keys — but it lives on your phone, so it is a "hot" wallet. It is convenient for DeFi and new tokens, while a hardware wallet is safer for large amounts.

## The setup most traders use

1. **Trading money** stays in your exchange wallet.
2. **Profits and long-term coins** move to a hardware wallet regularly.
3. **Your seed phrase** is written on paper (or metal) and stored offline — never in photos, email or cloud notes.

## How to move crypto safely

- Send a **small test amount** first.
- Always choose the **same network** on both sides (for example BTC on Bitcoin network, USDT on TRC20).
- Double-check the first and last characters of the address.
- Turn on a **withdrawal whitelist** on your exchange.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best crypto exchange wallet?
Binance and Bybit offer the most complete exchange wallets, with a built-in Web3 wallet. Bitunix is great for moving funds quickly between spot and futures.

### Is it safe to keep crypto on an exchange?
Large, well-run exchanges use strong security, but you are trusting the exchange. Keep only what you trade on the exchange.

### What happens if I lose my hardware wallet?
You can restore your funds on a new device using your recovery phrase — which is why keeping it safe is essential.
