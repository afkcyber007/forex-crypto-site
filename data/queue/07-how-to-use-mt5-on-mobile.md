MetaTrader 5 (MT5) is the most popular trading platform in the world, and its free mobile app lets you trade forex, gold and indices from your phone. This guide takes you from download to your first trade.

## Step 1: Install the MT5 app

Search for **"MetaTrader 5"** by MetaQuotes in the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and install it. It is free.

## Step 2: Log in to your broker account

1. Open your broker's client area and find your **MT5 login number**, **password** and **server name**.
2. In the MT5 app, tap **Settings → New account** (or **Manage accounts → +**).
3. Search for your broker's name and choose the **exact server** shown in your client area.
4. Enter your login and password and tap **Sign in**.

Brokers in our [best forex broker](/best-forex-broker/) ranking — such as [Exness](/brokers/exness/), [Vantage](/brokers/vantage/), [IC Markets](/brokers/ic-markets/) and [HFM](/brokers/hfm/) — all support MT5.

## Step 3: Add symbols to your quotes

Tap **Quotes → +** and search for the markets you want, such as **XAUUSD** (gold), **EURUSD**, **US30** or **NAS100**. Symbol names can differ slightly between brokers (for example XAUUSDm).

## Step 4: Read the chart

- Tap a symbol → **Chart**.
- Pinch to zoom; swipe to scroll back in time.
- Tap the **clock icon** to change the timeframe (M1, M5, M15, H1, H4, D1).
- Tap **f** to add indicators such as Moving Average or RSI.

## Step 5: Place a trade

1. Tap a symbol → **Trade**.
2. Choose the **volume** (lot size) — start with **0.01**.
3. Set your **Stop Loss** and **Take Profit** prices.
4. Tap **Buy by Market** or **Sell by Market**.

To place a pending order, change **Market Execution** to **Buy Limit**, **Sell Limit**, **Buy Stop** or **Sell Stop** and enter the price.

## Step 6: Manage and close trades

Open the **Trade** tab to see open positions and your profit or loss. Long-press a position to **modify** its stop loss and take profit, or to **close** it.

## MT5 mobile tips

- **Always set a stop loss** before you open the trade.
- Turn on **push notifications** in Settings to get alerts.
- Use a **demo account** first — tap **Open a demo account** in the app.
- Check your margin level in the **Trade** tab before adding new positions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the MT5 app free?
Yes, the app is free. You only pay normal trading costs to your broker.

### Why can't I find my broker's server?
Type the broker name exactly, or the server name shown in your client area. Some brokers have several servers — choose the one listed for your account.

### Can I use MT4 accounts in the MT5 app?
No. MT4 and MT5 accounts are separate. Open an MT5 account with your broker to use the MT5 app.
