If you are new to forex, the words **pip** and **lot** appear everywhere. Understanding them is essential, because they decide how much money you make or lose on every trade.

## What is a pip?

A **pip** ("percentage in point") is the standard unit of price movement in forex.

- For most pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD) one pip is **0.0001** — the fourth decimal place. If EURUSD moves from 1.0850 to 1.0851, it moved one pip.
- For pairs with the Japanese yen (USDJPY, GBPJPY) one pip is **0.01** — the second decimal place.
- Many platforms show an extra digit (a **pipette**, one tenth of a pip): 1.08505.

## What is a lot?

A lot is the size of your trade.

| Lot type | Units of base currency | Lot size on MT4/MT5 |
|---|---|---|
| Standard lot | 100,000 | 1.00 |
| Mini lot | 10,000 | 0.10 |
| Micro lot | 1,000 | 0.01 |

## How much is one pip worth?

For pairs where the US dollar is the second currency (EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD):

| Lot size | Value per pip |
|---|---|
| 1.00 lot | $10 |
| 0.10 lot | $1 |
| 0.01 lot | $0.10 |

**Example:** you buy 0.10 lot of EURUSD at 1.0850 and close at 1.0880. That is 30 pips × $1 = **$30 profit**.

For other pairs the pip value changes slightly with the exchange rate. Your trading platform shows it automatically.

## Pips on gold (XAUUSD)

Gold is usually quoted in dollars and cents, and one standard lot is 100 ounces. The simplest way to think about it: **with 1.00 lot, every $1 move in gold = $100**; with 0.01 lot, every $1 move = $1.

## Using pips to manage risk

1. Decide how much you will risk — for example 1% of a $500 account = **$5**.
2. Place your stop loss where the trade idea is wrong — for example **25 pips** away.
3. Position size = $5 ÷ 25 pips = **$0.20 per pip** = **0.02 lots** on EURUSD.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is 1 pip on 0.01 lot?
About $0.10 on EURUSD, GBPUSD and other USD-quoted pairs.

### How many pips does EURUSD move per day?
It varies, but EURUSD often moves around 50–100 pips in a normal day.

### Is a pip the same as a point?
On 5-digit platforms one "point" is one pipette (0.1 pip). Ten points = one pip.
