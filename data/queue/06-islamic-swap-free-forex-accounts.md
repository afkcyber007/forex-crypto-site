Many traders want to trade forex and gold in a way that follows Islamic finance principles, which prohibit earning or paying interest (riba). That is why many brokers offer **Islamic accounts**, also called **swap-free accounts**.

## What is a swap?

When you keep a forex or CFD position open overnight, the broker normally charges or pays a **swap** (also called rollover). It is based on the interest-rate difference between the two currencies. Because a swap works like interest, it is a concern for many Muslim traders.

## What is an Islamic (swap-free) account?

An Islamic account **removes overnight swaps**. You can hold positions overnight without paying or receiving interest-based charges. Everything else — spreads, platforms, leverage options — usually stays the same.

## Are there any fees instead of swaps?

Some brokers charge a **fixed administration fee** if a swap-free position stays open for a number of days, or apply wider spreads on certain instruments. These rules differ between brokers, so always read the account terms.

## How to open an Islamic account

1. Register with your broker using the **Visit Site** button on its review page.
2. Complete verification.
3. Choose the **swap-free / Islamic** option during account opening, or request it from the client area or support.
4. Check that your account shows as swap-free before holding positions overnight.

## Tips for swap-free traders

- Check whether **gold (XAUUSD)** and **indices** are swap-free on your account — some brokers only include major forex pairs.
- Read the rules about **administration fees** for long-held positions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is forex trading halal?
Scholars hold different views. Many consider spot currency exchange with immediate settlement and no interest acceptable. Ask a qualified scholar you trust.

### Is an Islamic account more expensive?
Sometimes slightly, through administration fees or spreads on specific instruments. Compare terms between brokers.

### Can non-Muslims open an Islamic account?
Some brokers allow it, others restrict swap-free accounts to residents of certain countries.
